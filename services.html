How this workshop ships

One repository, one CI, one audit surface. Sub-project histories were merged with git subtree, so their full commit history remains intact under projects/…. The portal's security workflow scans every push for leaked tokens, non-HTTPS resources and broken internal references.

Want one of these services on your own machine or LAN? Everything here is MIT — fork it, self-host it, ship it. The portal's deploy kit (Proxmox LXC + nginx/Caddy + Let’s Encrypt) covers self-hosting end-to-end, and Legitimacy & Ethics applies to everything we publish.
