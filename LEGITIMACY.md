# High on Intelligence — Legitimacy & Ethics Policy (Aethric Sovereign Publishing Standard)

High on Intelligence — Hazem Soussi · The Thinking Machine, published under Aethric Sovereign Publishing.

## 1. What this document guarantees
1. **Content legitimacy.** Every resource linked from the High on Intelligence Library is a legal, publicly accessible destination operated by its rightful owner (universities, foundations, companies, authors, governments). No pirated material, no cracked content, no scraped closures.
2. **Attribution.** All third-party material is credited to its authors. High on Intelligence curates; it does not steal.
3. **Hands-on security, legally.** Security tracks (TryHackMe, picoCTF, PortSwigger, OverTheWire, HTB) teach offensive techniques exclusively inside legal, sandboxed environments. Never apply these skills to systems you do not own or lack written permission to test.
4. **Health & spirit, responsibly.** Health content is general information from WHO, NHS, NIH and Harvard T.H. Chan — it is **not medical advice**. Consult a qualified physician. Spiritual texts belong to their traditions and are linked, not reinterpreted.
5. **Privacy by architecture.** The portal stores all progress, manifests and proposals in your browser's localStorage on your device. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no accounts, no data leaves your machine.
6. **No impersonation.** High on Intelligence is maintained by Hazem Soussi. Forks and mirrors must re-brand themselves and must not claim to be the original.
7. **Ethics of the thinking machine.** High on Intelligence celebrates human–machine collaboration, never machine domination of humans. No weaponized automation, no deception tooling, no surveillance-for-hire.

## 2. Acceptable use
- Fork it, mirror it, translate it (Arabic, French…) — keep attribution and this policy.
- Serve it on your own nodes/CDNs. You may not sell access to it.
- Do not misrepresent the legal status of security labs, or use them as cover for unauthorized access.

## 3. Reporting
- **Content or legal concern:** open an issue on the project's repository, or contact the maintainer. We will correct or remove within 30 days.
- **Security vulnerability in the portal itself:** see `.well-known/security.txt` (RFC 9116).

## 4. License
- High on Intelligence's own code and text: MIT-style — do what you want, keep the copyright notice and this policy.
- Third-party resources keep their own licenses. Fetching them from this page grants you nothing beyond what their owners grant.

## 5. What legitimacy is not
A self-signed certificate or a home-grown CA is **not** public legitimacy. On the public internet, legitimacy = a globally trusted certificate (Let's Encrypt or paid CA) + published policy (this file) + reachable legal contact. Inside our LAN and Proxmox, our own Aethric CA is the right tool — see `deploy/certs.md`.
